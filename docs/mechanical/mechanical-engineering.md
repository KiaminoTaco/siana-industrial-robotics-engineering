# Mechanical Engineering

The supplied mechanical project material covers the robot chassis and robotic-arm structure, including CAD assemblies, STEP/STL models, technical drawings, arm joints, supports, couplings, sheet-metal components and maintenance documentation.

Mechanical design is represented as a team-level contribution. The electrical contributor also provided supporting integration/research input, but the repository does not attribute the complete mechanical design to one person.

Authoritative design files are intentionally reserved for manual insertion:

```text
hardware/mechanical/cad/
hardware/mechanical/drawings/ 
```
